Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 (updated for crash reports)

Short version: your photos and videos never leave your iPhone. Broomly analyses them on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. The only thing Broomly sends is anonymous crash reports.

What Broomly accesses

To find what you can clean up, Broomly asks for access to your photo library. With your permission it reads your photos and videos and their details (such as date, size and dimensions) on your device. With Limited Access it can only see the photos you choose. You can change access at any time in Settings › Broomly › Photos.

What stays on your iPhone

Broomly stores the results of its scan on your device: for each item, a compact description used to find duplicates and similar shots, a sharpness score, its file size and date, plus your Keep list and settings. This data is never sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes it.

What Broomly does not do

  • It does not upload your photos, videos or any information about them.
  • It does not use advertising or tracking, and it does not profile you.
  • It does not create an account or read your contacts.
  • It does not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Feature requests

Settings › Feature Requests opens Broomly's public board on Canny in Safari. Broomly sends nothing to it; anything you post or vote there is between you and Canny, under Canny's privacy policy.

Deleting photos and videos

Broomly only deletes items after you confirm in the iOS system prompt. Deleted items move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.

iCloud Photos

If you use iCloud Photos, Apple's Photos framework may load a preview of an item from iCloud so you can see it in Broomly. That happens between your iPhone and Apple, under Apple's privacy policy; Broomly receives nothing.

Crash and performance reports

To find and fix bugs, Broomly sends crash reports, reports of the app freezing, and a sample of performance measurements to Sentry, an error-monitoring service. They contain technical details only: the error and code location, app version, iPhone model, iOS version and memory or disk state at the time. They never contain your photos, screenshots of the app, file names, your library, your IP address or anything that identifies you. Sentry's policy: sentry.io/privacy. If you share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may also send us anonymous crash reports.

Children

Broomly does not ask for personal information. Feedback is optional, and children should not include personal details in it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
