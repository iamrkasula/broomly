Support

Need help? Check the answers below, or email iamrkasula@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Scanning

How long does the first scan take?

About 10 minutes for a library of 30,000 photos and videos, sometimes longer on very large libraries. Broomly first reads your library ("preparing"), then looks at each item. Later scans only check what's new and take seconds.

Do I need to keep the app open?

The scan keeps going for a short while after you leave the app, then pauses. It carries on from where it stopped the next time you open Broomly; nothing is lost.

Why did the scan slow down?

When your iPhone gets warm, Broomly slows down by itself to let it cool, then speeds up again. Charging and other heavy apps can make this happen sooner.

Are photos in iCloud scanned?

Broomly never downloads your library. Items stored only in iCloud ("Optimize iPhone Storage") are compared using the preview kept on your iPhone.

Cleaning up

Where do deleted photos go?

To Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days. Nothing is deleted until you confirm in the iOS prompt.

Why aren't some photos selected?

In each group of duplicates or similar shots, Broomly keeps the best one. Favorites and edited photos are never pre-selected either. You can change any selection before deleting.

What is the Keep list?

Items you add to the Keep list are never suggested again. You can review or remove them in Settings › Keep list.

Is there a limit on how much I can delete?

No. Every feature is included, with no subscription, no trial and no daily limit.

Photo access

Broomly says it needs access to my photos

Open Settings › Broomly › Photos and choose Full Access. With Limited Access, Broomly only scans the photos you pick.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your photos are analysed on your iPhone and never uploaded; the app only sends anonymous crash reports. See the Privacy Policy.
